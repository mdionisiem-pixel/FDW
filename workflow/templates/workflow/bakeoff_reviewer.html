Reviewer check

In stage 4, a Claude reviewer fails any image that does not match the piece before you see it. This check runs that same reviewer, with the same instructions, on images you have already scored, and shows where it disagrees with you.

How your scores are read

An image counts as one you would accept when all four of your scores are {{ accept_score }} or higher; anything lower, as one you would reject. The number that matters most is how many images you would reject the reviewer also fails, because a missed one reaches buyers. Failing a good image only costs a redraw.
